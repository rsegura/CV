Interest classification: trusting agreement instead of a score

444 categories, confident mistakes. A LoRA-tuned bi-encoder proposes, a cross-encoder reranks, and only categories both rank in their top 5 survive. Human-judged strict precision 36% → 52%.

LoRA · Cross-encoders · vLLM → Platform
